Usage

Keep a quarter-emblem-width of clear space around the logo. Use the name without the tagline for narrow headers and the standalone symbol for launcher icons. Keep finished gloss, spacing and proportions intact. Use the prepared dark variant rather than CSS filters.

Colour

Violet #7046E8 · Ink #17152A · Lilac #B7A4FF · Paper #F7F5FF · White #FFFFFF.

The glossy finish is supplied as raster PNG artwork. The editable SVGs are separate flat silhouettes; they do not reproduce the glossy material. Larger icon exports are resized from the supplied raster master. Native-project and app-store validation remain part of implementation.

Developer handoff

Open README.md for the full instructions. Copy web/ to public/brand/, merge the head snippet and configure manifest paths. Import native resources into your own projects without replacing unrelated settings. QA-REPORT.json records export checks and ASSET-MANIFEST.json lists file hashes and image dimensions.
